Backtested results have inherent limitations. Such results do not represent the impact of material economic and market factors might have on an investment advisor's decision-making process if the advisor was actually managing client assets. Backtested performance also differs from actual performance because it is achieved through retroactive application of model portfolios designed with the benefit of hindsight. Backtested performance results assume the reinvestment of dividends and capital gains and rebalanced monthly to maintain the investment objective. The results are net of the effects of the underlying vehicle fees, estimated trading costs, and manager fees of 1.65%, billed monthly. For all data periods, annualized standard deviation is presented as an approximation by multiplying the monthly standard deviation by the square root of twelve. Annual data may differ materially from this estimate.
