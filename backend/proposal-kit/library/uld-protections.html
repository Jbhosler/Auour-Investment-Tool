ULD is not a bank deposit and is not FDIC insured. Its protections come from how the assets are owned, held and diversified, and from how the strategy manages risk.

How the Assets Are Held

You own the positions

ULD holds ETF shares in the client's own account. They are not assets of Auour and do not sit on Auour's balance sheet.

Fund assets kept separate

Each ETF's underlying securities are held by a custodian, apart from the fund sponsor's own assets.

An owner, not a creditor

A bank depositor is a creditor of the bank. A ULD investor owns the underlying investment positions.
