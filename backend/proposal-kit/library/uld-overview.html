Money Comes in Many Forms…
“Dear” Money
Dear money refers to funds that are immediately accessible and best suited for daily expenses, payments, and short-term financial buffers. These funds are typically held in bank checking and savings accounts to ensure quick access and security.
“Near” Money
Near money consists of highly liquid investments that offer greater income potential while maintaining flexibility. The Ultra Low Duration strategy can be an ideal placement for near money, as it provides a balance between liquidity and enhanced yield opportunities compared to traditional bank deposits.

Why Choose ULD?

Financial Stability
Attempts to mimic a bank’s focus on liquidity and safety.
Higher Returns
Aims for higher yields compared to typical bank returns.
Liquid Assets
No lockup restrictions, ensuring your funds are accessible when needed.
Diversification
Diversified across various short-term fixed-income markets.

The strategy targets investment segments traditionally accessible only to banks and institutional investors, striving for a disciplined and risk-conscious approach to financial stability and higher yields.
