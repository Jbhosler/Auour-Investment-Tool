Investment Strategy

The Ultra Low Duration Strategy is meant to produce a steady near-term income stream through interest and dividends. The assets are invested in a short duration exchange traded fund (ETF) portfolio, guided by a multi-factor regime model developed to participate in rising markets and provide downside protection in falling markets.

Auour Regime Model

At the core of the Instinct Strategies is the Auour Regime Model (ARM), a multi-factor model, which guides the asset allocation process to better align the portfolio for the current market environment. The regime model provides a highly analytic and rigorous process that looks at many global market factors to determine our view of the risk landscape. These factors include:

  • Fixed Income Credit Spreads
  • Asset Pricing Dynamics
  • Interaction of Global Markets
  • Asset Class Valuations

Based upon our proprietary analysis of these factors, ARM determines the regime of the various global markets. This enables us to monitor and change asset allocation across a spectrum from aggressive to very conservative. We believe this process represents the next generation of downside protection investment products.

An Investment Foundation

The Auour Instinct Strategies have been developed to provide a foundation for broad and diversified investment assets. The goal is to increase the purchasing power of the client's investment portfolios by focusing on those facets of investing that impact performance: market participation, asset allocation, and expense reduction. Through the use of the Instinct Strategies and the Auour Regime Model, we believe we offer a better investment alternative with lower volatility driving a better outcome for the client.
