Leveraging Institutional Experience

Founded in 2013, Auour has been built on over 60 years of institutional money management experience of its founders. The firm has leveraged this combined experience to drive a new paradigm in the management of clients' wealth. Focusing on lowering the total cost with the added benefit of protecting wealth during turbulent market environments, Auour offers a new perspective on asset management.

Designed from the Ground Up

Auour was designed on a clean sheet of paper without the inherent inefficiencies and self-dealing of more established firms. With the starting premise that more of the market's return needs to end with the client, the firm focuses on those aspects that have shown to most benefit the client; market participation, asset allocation, and expense reduction. Built off of industry research and the founders' own learnings of best practices, Auour is structured to align with these core elements of investing.

Combining Investment Strengths

Auour is unique in its combination of different investing disciplines. Rather than relying on only one, the firm leverages the strength of a deep analytical framework and over 40 years of fundamental investing to arrive at a combination that results in stability in process and durability in its analytical rigor. Where others fail by relying too much on one, Auour promotes the benefits of a combination that should last a lifetime.
