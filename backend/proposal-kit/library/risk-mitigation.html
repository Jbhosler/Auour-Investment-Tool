01

Investment markets goes through cycles

Markets tend to follow certain cycles. In those different stages of a cycle, or regimes, investments will react to changes in global economic activity. In some infrequent time periods, most assets will fall as risk is no longer tolerated. And cash is one of the few that can withstand the downturn.
02

Identify and adjust to the regime

Auour looks for changes in the global risk appetite. Identifying those changes allows us to adjust portfolios to favor assets that tend to do better in each of the regimes. Or when markets no longer warrant participation, we can move to cash, aiming to mitigate the losses static portfolios will likely experience.
03

Being proactive can mitigate downturns

Understanding the risk regime of the market allows Auour to be proactive and aiming to mitigate material and enduring market declines. It also provides the opportunity to better gauge the time to get back in and capture more of the market's potential.
